About Megara

Experiences,not just centrepieces.

We design every event custom, drawing on elements of your story to create an experience you could never have imagined, yet is so unmistakably yours. Our work is painstaking and precise — we work hard to make it look easy and effortless.

hi friend!

Founder & Lead Planner

I'm Meg, your new planning bestie.

Originally from Johannesburg, my love for Cape Town was cemented while studying at the University of Cape Town. Soon after graduating I joined the illustrious superyacht industry — drawn to its fast-paced, high-energy and demanding world, I quickly rose to Interior Manager on some of the world's most prestigious yachts.

Drawing on a decade of international VIP experience, I'm a dynamic leader and formidable planner, skilled at producing highly complex events with complete focus. My defining trait is the ability to problem-solve, so that every event is a truly customised experience.

With a calm demeanour, an approachable nature and sheer joy in creating something unique, I'm a trusted partner to colleagues, clients and vendors worldwide. I'm so proud of our gorgeous, passionate and hard-working team, who stop at nothing to create a fabulous experience for our clients.

My superpower

Helping my clients with a unique and creative vision — the difference between a good event and an incredible experience.

I look forward to meeting you soon! xo
