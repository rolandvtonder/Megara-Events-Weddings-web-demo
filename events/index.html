Good to know

Something else on your mind? Email enquiries@megara.co.za.

How far in advance should we start planning?+

Give yourself the gift of time. Allow at least a month before the event to begin sourcing quotes — larger productions can take several months. The more time we have, the more realistic it is to deliver your result within budget.

Can you work with our brand guidelines?+

Absolutely. Every activation and corporate event is designed around your brand, objectives and audience — we're as comfortable with a CI manual as a mood board.

Do you work outside Cape Town?+

Yes — we have delivered activations in both Johannesburg and Cape Town and travel wherever the event needs us.
